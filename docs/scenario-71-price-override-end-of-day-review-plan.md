# Scenario 71 — Price Override, Manual Installment Terms & End-of-Day Review — Gap Analysis & Closing Plan

**Source**: client document "Price Override" (two pages of AI-generated mockups plus four Q&A answers), relayed 2026-10-13. Developer instruction: take the **logic** from it, keep **our existing UX**, and evaluate before building. The mockups are reference only; where a mockup detail contradicts our data or code it is listed under "Where the client document conflicts with the system" below.

The four questions the client answered:

1. Where does Price Override live in the POS? On every cart line. The cashier enters a new price, picks a reason from a list, adds a short note; the total updates and the adjustment is recorded for accounting and audit. **No PIN or approval at the counter.**
2. Override on an in-house installment with no prior credit application? The cashier may **waive the credit application**. DP, term and MI then auto-populate from the price list, and all three stay editable.
3. "Good as cash in 60 days with 50% DP"? A new **FMI (first monthly installment) date** field. The cashier adjusts DP and MI and sets the FMI date 60 days out.
4. Override with **no down payment** (a friend of management)? The PDF stops at the question — no answer text. The review mockup shows a "DP waived" flag, so this plan assumes a 0 DP is allowed and flagged (see C4).

Everything an override produces lands in an **end-of-day review**: a manager approves or rejects each entry, then signs off the day with a PIN, after which the list is locked.

**Status**: all four parts built and tested 2026-10-13 → 14 on `fix/bank-accounts-single-cash-gl`; **uncommitted, nothing pushed**, no ClickUp ticket. See the Implementation Log at the end for what was done and what is left. The decisions below were settled with the developer on 2026-10-13 and supersede the proposed defaults they replace.

## The scenario we're building toward

1. **Counter.** The cashier rings up a sale. On any line they can change the price. They do not need a manager: they pick a **reason** and type a **note**, and the sale goes through.
2. **Installment.** For an in-house installment, the cashier can tick **Waive the credit application**. The plan fields fill from the price list (DP, term, MI) and the cashier can retype any of them. A new **First due date (FMI)** field sets when the first installment falls due. A one-tap preset covers "50% DP, balance in 60 days".
3. **Recorded.** The sale posts at the price actually charged. Each overridden line (and each line with typed terms or a waived credit application) leaves a **review entry** with the list price, the override, the plan, the reason and note, and flags.
4. **End of day.** A manager opens **Override Review**, approves or rejects each entry (or approves all), enters their PIN and signs off the day. A signed-off day is locked.

## What's already done ✅

1. **A per-line price override exists, gated by a manager PIN.** `pos/checkout/_components/PriceOverrideDialog.tsx` takes a new price and a PIN; `handlePriceOverrideApprove` (`pos/checkout/page.tsx` ~3054-3076) writes it onto the line; the line shows "Overridden" or "No price — Override" (~5080-5100). The backend re-checks the manager holds `pos:transactions:price_override` (`transactions.service.ts` ~993-1010) and stores only `PosTransactionLine.priceOverrideBy`.
2. **Price Use / price list with DP and MI per term.** `PriceListItem.downPayment`, `PriceListItemTerm.monthlyInstallment` / `ppd`. Checkout resolves a price per line under its own Price Use.
3. **Installment plan math is one shared function.** `installment-calculation.util.ts` (`computeInstallmentPreview`, `buildCuratedInstallmentPreview`) feeds both the live preview (`financing-terms.service.ts` ~178-216) and the posted schedule (`transactions.service.ts` ~4717-4729). The linked installment account derives its factor from the schedule's **actual** monthly (`createLinkedInstallmentAccount`), so a typed MI would reach the ledger without further changes there.
4. **A proven precedent for "flagged sale that waives the credit-application and down-payment gates"**: Employee Appliance Loan (Scenario 60) and X-Deal (Scenario 67) — `skipsCreditApplication` / `downPaymentWaived` in checkout (`page.tsx` ~1565-1574) and the matching skips in `validateAndPrepare` (`transactions.service.ts` ~440-472, ~540, ~783-791).
5. **Manager approval of installment sales already exists** (`release-form-requests.service.ts`): every installment sale from a submitter without `pos:transaction:override` is held for a manager first.
6. **Approval-list and sign-off patterns to copy**: Release Approvals (`/pos/release-approvals`), manager-PIN-only identification (`cashier-pin.controller.ts` `…/validate-pin-only`), the Daily Collection report's Prepared / Checked / Certified sign-offs, and the audit log.

## What's not done / gaps ❌⚠️

1. **No reason, no note.** Neither is captured anywhere. There is no reason list.
2. **The list price is lost on override.** `handlePriceOverrideApprove` sets `priceListItemId: null` and `priceListDownPayment: null`, so the sale keeps no record of what the price would have been. "% below list" cannot be computed afterwards. It also drops the rate-card link, so an overridden installment line falls back to the 10% DP floor and the generic factor-rate MI instead of the rate card.
3. **The PIN is the whole control, and the client wants it gone.** Today a cashier cannot override without a manager present. The new rule is after-the-fact review, so the permission model changes (who may apply, who may review).
4. **No manual DP / term / MI for in-house.** MI comes from the factor rate or the rate card; the term must be a `FinancingTerm` row (the dev seed has only 3, 6, 9 and 12 months); a down payment is locked to the rate card's figure where one exists (`transactions.service.ts` ~761-774) and floored at 10% otherwise. A 0 DP is blocked for everything except employee loans and X-Deals. `InstallmentSchedule.financingTermId` is a **required** FK.
5. **No FMI date.** The first due date is always sale date + 1 month (`buildLines` in `installment-calculation.util.ts`). Nothing lets the cashier set it.
6. **No credit-application waiver for ordinary sales.** `creditApplicationId` is required for every in-house installment sale outside the employee-loan, X-Deal and government cases (~540).
7. **No review entries.** Nothing records an override as a reviewable item; there is no sign-off table, no review page and no sidebar entry.
8. **No threshold for "over 10% below list".** `PosConfig.discountOverrideThreshold` (default 20%) is for discounts and drives a PIN, a different thing.
9. **Offline.** The offline queue already drops sale-level flags (Scenario 67 R7). A reason, a note or a waiver entered offline would be lost.

## Closing the gaps — proposed parts

Each part gets its own tests and manual test steps and stops for confirmation before the next begins (house convention). Schema changes are reported, not applied.

### Part 1 — Capture the override (reason, note, list price; no PIN)

**Backend**

- New `PosOverrideReason` (tenant lookup: `name`, `isActive`, `displayOrder`) with list/create/update endpoints and the seeded starter list (D6). Never hard-deleted — entries keep pointing at it.
- `PosTransaction`: `exceptionReasonId String?`, `exceptionNote String? @db.Text`.
- `PosTransactionLine`: `listUnitPrice Decimal?` — written by the server from the line's `PriceListItem` (D9). `priceOverrideBy` is kept and now means **who applied** the override (it used to mean the approving manager) — noted in the schema comment.
- `PosConfig.priceOverrideFlagPct Decimal @default(10)` (D8).
- `validateAndPrepare`: remove the `managerOverride` / `managerUserId` requirement for a price override (~993-1010). In its place: the submitter must hold the apply permission, and `exceptionReasonId` + `exceptionNote` are required whenever any line has `priceOverride`. An unknown or inactive reason is rejected.
- Seed + backfill script for the permission changes in D3; the new `pos:override-review` resource is added in Part 4 but its seed row can land here.

**Frontend**

- `PriceOverrideDialog`: drop the PIN field and `validatePriceOverrideByPin`; keep the price input and the "currently resolved at" hint.
- `handlePriceOverrideApprove` → `applyPriceOverride`: **keep** `priceListItemId` on the line (D9).
- New **Exception** section in the checkout panel (flat, no box, full width): Reason select + Note, shown while any exception is active, required before Confirm. The cart line keeps its "Overridden" mark and gains "Clear override".
- Offline: the override action is disabled with a one-line reason (D17).
- Settings: a small section on POS Settings to manage the reason list.

**As built (Part 1, 2026-10-13)** — where it differs from the bullets above:

- **No permission anywhere** (L1 supersedes D3): the price-override check is gone from `validateAndPrepare` and nothing replaces it, so there is no seed change and no backfill. `userHasPriceOverridePermission` was deleted (nothing else called it). The `…/price-override/validate-pin*` endpoints stay but nothing in the app calls them now. Reading the reasons needs `pos:transactions:create`; changing them needs `pos:config:manage` — both existing.
- **D9 changed.** The frontend does **not** keep `priceListItemId` on an overridden line: that would keep the rate-card down-payment lock and curated monthly attached to a price that no longer matches them, which is Part 2's job. Instead the line carries a new field `overriddenFromPriceListItemId`; the server validates it (tenant, the line's item, the line's Price Use) and reads the list price off that row into `listUnitPrice`. - **`PosConfig.priceOverrideFlagPct` (D8) moved to Part 4**, where the flag is first used.

- **An override on a row of several units of one serialized item applies to every unit** in the row (the row shows one price). Before, only the first line took it.
- Frontend line state `priceOverrideBy` / `priceOverrideApproverName` became `priceOverridden` (no approver exists any more). The reason and note ride along when a sale is parked, resumed or taken to raise a credit application.

### Part 2 — Typed installment terms, FMI date, 60-day preset

**Backend**

- `PosTransactionLine`: `manualPlan Boolean @default(false)`, `manualTermMonths Int?`, `manualMonthlyInstallment Decimal?`, `firstDueDate DateTime?`. The line's existing `downPayment` carries the DP.
