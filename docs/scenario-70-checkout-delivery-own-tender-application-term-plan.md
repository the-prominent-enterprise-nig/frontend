# Scenario 70 — Checkout: delivery last, with its own payment method; the credit application's term wins on return

**Source**: client feedback relayed 2026-10-12, in four notes:

> **Checkout**
>
> - Delivery should be at the bottom and should have its own payment method.
> - Separate JE.
> - Item total, Delivery Fee, TOTAL w/ delivery fee — but it should be visible at the receipt that item total and del fee are separate.
> - Make sure whatever is in the Credit Application is the one that should appear in the checkout.

The last note, clarified by the developer the same day: if checkout was on 5 months, then the credit application was decided on 12, going back to checkout it should be **12**.

Builds on Scenario 66 (delivery fee on its own collection receipt) and **partly reverses Scenario 68** (D3 / D10: the fee rides on the sale's tender). Frontend only. No backend, schema or migration change: the backend already accepts a fee with its own tender, its own CR and its own journal entry.

**Branch**: worked on the current branch in the frontend (`feat/tax-setup-vat-wht`), uncommitted. Nothing committed or pushed.

## What was built

1. **Delivery moved down the panel, and made impossible to miss.** `DeliverySection` now renders after the Order Summary and above the Total (`checkout/page.tsx`), no longer above the Order Summary. Developer feedback the same day: the collapsed row (a 13px icon, a small caps label and a 16px checkbox) was too small to see in a long panel, so the toggle is now a full-width card: a round truck icon, a bold "Delivery" heading in the brand purple, a line saying what it does, a 24px checkbox, and a solid purple state once ticked. The whole card is the click target. The Delivery fee field was also too small and right-aligned (it had borrowed the register's money-field style), so it is now larger (16px text, 52px high against the 34px of the other fields), written from the left, with a ₱ in front; measured and checked from a screenshot.
2. **The Total moved to the very bottom, with the delivery fee as a note.** The "Total" that sat above Amount received in the payment box is gone. A totals block now sits right after the Delivery section and above the payment fields: the **Total** (the sale's part collected at this register, the same figure the old payment-box Total showed; labelled "Total due now" when it is less than the sale, e.g. a down payment), and under it the delivery fee as a note ("collected on its own CR, not part of the sale"). The fee is never added into the Total. A **Copy** button sits beside the Total (`checkout/_components/CopyAmountButton.tsx`): it puts the amount on the clipboard as a plain number (`6190.00`, no ₱ or commas, so it pastes into Amount received, a number input), shows "Copied" for 1.5 seconds and a toast, and falls back to the old selection-based copy when the clipboard API is not available. It copies the Total only, never the fee. Test ids: `checkout-grand-total`, `checkout-fee-total` (the old `payment-total` is gone).
3. **The fee's own Paid with, always.**
   - Removed `deliveryFeeWithSale`, `collectTarget` and `deliveryWithSaleTender()`. The payment box's Total, Amount received, change and "Underpaid" now use `tenderTarget` (the sale only).
   - `DeliverySection` lost its `feeFollowsSale` prop and always shows the Paid with picker (Cash on Hand, Check, Bank Transfer, GCash, Billers, QR, Debit/Credit Card) with its details, plus the Delivery CR No.
   - The old "paid with the sale's payment" note and the payment box's "Includes the delivery fee…" line are gone. When the items collect nothing the box says so and points down to the Delivery section.
   - The success screen's tender label for the fee is now the fee's own tender.
4. **The delivery fee is a note, never part of the Total.**
   - Order Summary: the Total is the sale's (`totalAmount`); with a fee, a note line under it: "Delivery fee — collected on its own CR, not part of the sale".
   - Success screen: "Sale total", any financed / billed amount, then "Delivery fee (own CR, not part of the sale)" as a `+` line, then Paid Now (collected, fee included). No "Total w/ delivery fee" anywhere.
5. **The Order Summary was made bigger and clearer** (developer feedback, 2026-10-12: it was too small to read at the till). Heading 14px bold in the brand purple; the cart card has a larger header and rows; the item name is 16px (was 12px), the SKU and serial text 12px (was 10px), the VAT badge 11px (was 9px); the Price Use picker uses the regular size instead of the tiny pill; the quantity − / + buttons are 40px (were 24px) with a 18px quantity; the line price is 18px bold (was 12px); the remove X is always visible and a larger target (it used to appear only on hover, which a touch screen never shows); Subtotal and VAT lines are one size up; the cart scrolls from 28rem instead of 18rem. Layout and behaviour are unchanged. Checked in a 6-check browser run (no DB writes) and from a screenshot.
6. **Credit application term wins on return** (`checkout/page.tsx`, the re-apply effect). It used to re-apply an application's terms only when a line had **no down payment** yet. A cart restored from the till-to-application detour already had one, so it kept its own term. It now also applies when a line's term differs from the application's. It still runs once per application + line count, so the cashier's later change is left alone.

Files: `checkout/page.tsx`, `checkout/_components/DeliverySection.tsx`, `checkout/_utils/delivery.ts`, `e2e/pos-checkout-delivery.spec.ts`.

## Worth flagging

- **Left in the dev database, from the throwaway runs, for the developer to clear**: 8 `loyalty_accounts` rows and 8 `accounting_audit_logs` rows (action `CREATE`, resource `crm:customer`). Each is one throwaway customer called `ZZ S70 Tmp <timestamp>` created through the API on 2026-10-12 between about 11:15 and 11:22 (Manila time). The customers themselves and both kinds of application fixture were deleted; creating a customer through the API also writes those two rows and deleting the customer does not remove them. (An attempt to remove them was blocked by the session's permission classifier, and was not worked around.) Nothing else from these runs remains.
- **Reverses Scenario 68 D3 / D10.** The client may later ask for "one payment" again. The old behaviour is in `git` history of `checkout/page.tsx` (`deliveryFeeWithSale`, `collectTarget`, `deliveryWithSaleTender`).
- **"Total w/ delivery fee" is gone, by the developer's decision.** The client's original note asked for "Item total, Delivery Fee, TOTAL w/ delivery fee"; the developer's reading is that the Total is the sale's and the fee is only a note (a collection, not a sale). Worth confirming with the client. The two figures are still separate and visible on the success screen and in Transaction Detail, which is what the note's "visible at the receipt" asked for.
- **The cashier no longer takes sale + fee as one lump.** They collect the sale in the payment box and the fee in the Delivery section, so two amounts are handed over. That is what a separate payment method means.
- **Loose by decision**: the application's term can still be changed at checkout and the server does not compare it with the application. If the client later wants it locked, the server check is the place (`TransactionsService.validateAndPrepare`, next to the exact item-set check).
- **Not changed**: the application's own figures other than the term and down payment (price, quantity, monthly, total payable, LCP, first due date, PPD) are still not carried into checkout; they were previews by design.
- The Daily Collection Report, session close and GL are untouched: the fee was already on its own receipt and entry.
